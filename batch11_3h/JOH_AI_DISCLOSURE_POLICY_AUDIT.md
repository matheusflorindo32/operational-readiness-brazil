# OUP journal AI-use and disclosure audit

## Live policy source

https://academic.oup.com/pages/for-authors/journals/preparing-and-submitting-your-manuscript/ai-principles-for-oxford-journals/policy-on-ai-use-and-disclosure-for-oxford-journals-authors

Access revalidated: 2026-10-11. The current OUP journals policy says that language-only clarity improvements and reference formatting may be permitted without disclosure; substantial editing, rewriting, reorganization, translation, data work, code, or generated illustrative material typically require disclosure. It requires disclosed AI use in both the cover letter and the manuscript acknowledgements, with tool/version, use, and author review/validation. It also states that authors remain accountable and that AI tools do not qualify as authors.
