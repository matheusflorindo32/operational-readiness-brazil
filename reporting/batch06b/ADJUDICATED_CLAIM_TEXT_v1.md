# ADJUDICATED CLAIM TEXT v1 — Reviewer 1

## BR-CLM-001
In one local sample of 22 military police officers in Acre, caloric and protein intake during day shifts were associated with selected psychomotor-vigilance metrics; these observational associations do not establish dietary causality.

## BR-CLM-002
In one regional observational sample of 34 military police officers, day shift, night shift and off-duty periods were associated with differences in energy/macronutrient intake and meal timing; the findings should not be generalized to all Brazilian police.

## BR-CLM-003 — PENDING_REVIEWER2
Within the specific induced-stress protocol applied to 24 PMES cadets, most measured shooting outcomes did not significantly worsen; this result derives from a cohort family with PARTIAL_OVERLAP with EV-1474 and should not be generalized to operational stress.

## BR-CLM-004 — PENDING_REVIEWER2
In one cross-sectional analysis of 26 PMES cadets, overall physical fitness did not consistently explain shooting proficiency; only selected associations were observed, and the cohort has PARTIAL_OVERLAP with EV-1473.

## BR-CLM-005 — PENDING_REVIEWER2
PMMG records document operational tourniquet use but do not establish patient-outcome effectiveness.

## BR-CLM-006 — PENDING_REVIEWER2
In a staggered difference-in-differences evaluation across 2,355 municipalities in nine Brazilian states, a results-based/data-driven policing reform was associated with an estimated average reduction of 18.3 homicides per 100,000 inhabitants; this is a public-safety policy outcome, not an individual physiological-readiness outcome.

## BR-CLM-007
The Escola Segura evaluation reported no impact on the analyzed outcomes.

## BR-CLM-008
In a nonrandomized study of 34 cadets, supplemental martial-arts experience was associated with better simulated use-of-force performance; the simulated outcome does not establish real-world operational effectiveness.

## BR-CLM-009
Among 219 male PMTO recruits, six months of academy training was associated with broad improvements in physical-fitness measures; without an external control group, the gains cannot be causally attributed to specific training components.

## BR-CLM-010 — CONTEXT ONLY / PENDING_REVIEWER2
In 20 male Brazilian Army peacekeepers in Haiti, operational patrol was accompanied by body-mass and autonomic changes consistent with hydration/physiological strain; this military evidence is not direct evidence for Brazilian police or firefighters.

## BR-CLM-011 — PENDING_REVIEWER2
In a cross-sectional Paraná cohort of 7,968 public-safety personnel (6,621 police officers and 1,347 firefighters), selected health conditions were associated with medical non-readiness and women were included; findings are state-specific and noncausal, do not establish national prevalence, and have limited female representation.
