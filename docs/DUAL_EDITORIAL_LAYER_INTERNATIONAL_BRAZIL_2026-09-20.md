## Permanent rule

From this phase onward, every relevant scientific product must maintain two parallel layers whenever evidence is sufficient:

1. INTERNATIONAL / GENERAL SCIENTIFIC VERSION
2. BRAZILIAN PUBLIC SAFETY ADAPTATION

The Brazil layer is an adaptation, not a translation.

## International layer

Scope: tactical athlete; military; law enforcement; firefighters; first responders; operational readiness; occupational readiness; TCCC/TECC; implementation science; international applicability.

## Brazil layer

Scope: Polícia Militar; Polícia Civil; Polícia Penal; Corpos de Bombeiros Militares; Guardas Municipais when relevant; Forças Armadas when transferable; special operations; patrol and specialized policing; occupational exposure; shifts and duty schedules; uniforms/body armor/equipment; Brazilian climate and regional differences; SUS/SAMU/APH; occupational health; training reality; institutional resources; implementation capacity.

## Brazil evidence statuses

- BRAZIL_CORE_EVIDENCE
- BRAZIL_SUPPORTING_EVIDENCE
- BRAZIL_CONTEXTUAL_EVIDENCE
- BRAZIL_CORE_CANDIDATE
- BRAZIL_LOW_PRIORITY
- BRAZIL_PROPOSED_EXCLUDE

These are additive to the main scientific tier.

## Claim provenance

Every important claim must be tagged as one of:
- INTERNATIONAL_CLAIM
- BRAZIL_SPECIFIC_CLAIM
- INTERNATIONAL_EVIDENCE_EXTRAPOLATED_TO_BRAZIL

International evidence must never be represented as Brazilian evidence.

## Transferability categories

- HIGH_TRANSFERABILITY
- MODERATE_TRANSFERABILITY
- LOW_TRANSFERABILITY
- REQUIRES_BRAZILIAN_VALIDATION
- NOT_TRANSFERABLE_WITHOUT_ADAPTATION

## Framework rule

Maintain an international operational-readiness framework and a proposed Brazilian adaptation for public safety.
The Brazil framework must be labeled: PROPOSED BRAZILIAN ADAPTATION — NOT YET VALIDATED until formal validation exists.

## Manuscript rule

Maintain progressively:
- MANUSCRIPT_PRE_REVIEW_vX.X.docx
- MANUSCRIPT_PRE_REVIEW_BRAZIL_vX.X.docx

The Brazil manuscript must be scientifically adapted, not merely translated.

## Human gate

AI_QUALIFIED and HUMAN_CLAIM_READY remain separate. No human approval is simulated.
