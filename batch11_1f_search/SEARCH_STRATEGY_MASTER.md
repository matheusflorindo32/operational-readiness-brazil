# Batch 11.1F Search Strategy

Four PubMed/MEDLINE targeted, free-full-text discovery queries were executed for fitness, sleep, tactical medicine, and workload gaps. Subscription databases were documented as unavailable; no access was implied. Records were deduplicated against the canonical 1,484 universe. This batch performed discovery and title/abstract screening only; no external record was admitted without lawful full text, result-level extraction, appraisal, integrity review, overlap control, and human materiality review.
