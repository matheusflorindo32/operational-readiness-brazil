# Brazil reconstruction blueprint

## Title

**Evidence boundaries for selected readiness domains in Brazilian public safety: an evidence-informed integrative synthesis**

## Article type

Evidence-informed integrative synthesis with Brazilian public-safety adaptation; not a systematic review.

## Central question

Which result-located findings from Brazilian public-safety settings can inform a bounded readiness evidence architecture without generalizing local observations to all institutions?

## Primary objective

Synthesize bounded Brazilian evidence on cardiometabolic surveillance, task-specific performance null findings, tactical-medical record use, organizational evaluation and implementation context.

## Sections

1. Scope, evidence roles and transfer boundaries.
2. Cardiometabolic surveillance in sampled police cohorts.
3. Task-specific shooting findings and overlap control.
4. Tactical-medicine and organizational evidence: descriptive/contextual use only.
5. Null results, limitations and research gaps.
6. Proposed framework role and future validation.

## Included domains

Cardiometabolic surveillance; shooting/tactical performance; tactical medicine as descriptive use; organizational/implementation context; musculoskeletal pain as contextual evidence.

## Removed domains

National prevalence claims, causal role/shift effects, validated readiness score, clinical effectiveness of APH-tactical policies, universal nutrition/fitness prescriptions, and definitive mental-health prediction.

## Framework role

`PROPOSED SYNTHESIS FRAMEWORK — NOT YET VALIDATED`

## Target length and references

3,000–4,000 words; working reference range 8–15 after human review. This is a planning range, not a target.

## Limitation

All claim decisions remain AI-provisional and human review is required before final reference inclusion.
