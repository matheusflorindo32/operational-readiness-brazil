# International reconstruction blueprint

## Title

**Evidence boundaries for selected operational-readiness domains in tactical populations: an evidence-informed integrative synthesis**

## Article type

Evidence-informed integrative synthesis with an explicit evidence-governance framework; not a systematic review.

## Central question

What bounded, result-located evidence can inform selected operational-readiness domains in tactical populations without treating readiness as a validated composite score?

## Primary objective

Synthesize result-located evidence for sleep-loss caffeine mitigation, cardiometabolic surveillance, and task-specific performance boundaries while preserving null findings and limits of transferability.

## Sections

1. Scope and evidence-governance method.
2. Bounded sleep-loss caffeine evidence.
3. Cardiometabolic surveillance and task-specific performance boundaries.
4. Null findings, cohort overlap and transferability.
5. Structural gaps and proposed framework role.
6. Limitations and future research.

## Included domains

Sleep/fatigue/recovery; cardiometabolic surveillance; shooting/tactical performance as supporting evidence; tactical-medicine and organizational material as discussion context only.

## Removed domains

Composite operational-readiness score, universal nutrition claims, physical/academy-readiness claims, hydration/heat, definitive mental-health prediction, and clinical-effectiveness tactical medicine.

## Framework role

`PROPOSED SYNTHESIS FRAMEWORK — NOT YET VALIDATED`

## Target length and references

3,500–4,500 words; working reference range 10–18 after human review. This is a planning range, not a target.

## Limitation

All claim decisions remain AI-provisional and human review is required before final reference inclusion.
