## Purpose

This manuscript is the Brazil-specific companion layer to the international operational-readiness project. It is not a direct translation of the international manuscript. Its purpose is to test how international tactical-athlete and operational-readiness evidence may or may not transfer to Brazilian public-safety organizations.

## Brazilian scope

The intended scope includes, when supported by evidence: Polícia Militar; Polícia Civil; Polícia Penal; Corpos de Bombeiros Militares; Guardas Municipais; selected military evidence when transferable; specialized and special-operations units.

## Core Brazilian adaptation domains

1. Individual physical capacity
2. Occupational task demand
3. External load and equipment
4. Environmental stress
5. Sleep, fatigue and duty schedules
6. Musculoskeletal health and return to duty
7. Cardiovascular and metabolic health
8. Mental health and stress
9. Nutrition and hydration
10. Cognitive performance and decision-making
11. Tactical medicine / APH / TCCC / TECC
12. Organizational readiness and implementation
13. SUS / SAMU / public-safety interface
14. Regional and institutional heterogeneity

## Evidence status

At v0.1, the international evidence base is more developed than the Brazil-specific evidence base.
Any statement derived from international studies must be marked as INTERNATIONAL_EVIDENCE_EXTRAPOLATED_TO_BRAZIL until corresponding Brazilian evidence is identified and qualified.
