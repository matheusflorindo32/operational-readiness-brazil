# BRAZIL B2 — FINAL NARROWED BLUEPRINT

## Title

**Evidence boundaries in Brazilian public-safety readiness: a bounded evidence-informed integrative synthesis**

## Central question and objective

What do result-located Brazilian public-safety sources support about medical readiness, task-specific performance, tactical medicine and organizational context? The objective is to synthesize the available evidence while retaining null findings, cohort limits and institutional transferability constraints.

## Article type

Evidence-informed integrative synthesis; not a systematic review. The `PROPOSED SYNTHESIS FRAMEWORK — NOT YET VALIDATED` is conceptual only.

## Claims and structure

- Core: bounded cardiometabolic surveillance context; medical non-readiness associations.
- Supporting: shooting null findings, tourniquet-use record review, Escola Segura null evaluation.
- Contextual: institutional implementation case and two cross-sectional musculoskeletal observations, including EV-1467.
- Sections: medical readiness; task-specific and tactical-medicine evidence; organizational/implementation context; limitations and future validation.
- Removed from core: nutrition/supplementation, physical fitness/academy readiness, hydration/heat.

## Density and viability

Nine result IDs across seven independent source families support a **bounded full manuscript** (estimated 3,000–4,000 words). Citations are working-only and human review remains pending.

## Limitations

Evidence is heterogeneous and mostly observational or descriptive; the shooting publications overlap in one cohort; no national prevalence, causal, intervention-effect, framework-validation or global-readiness inference is permitted.
