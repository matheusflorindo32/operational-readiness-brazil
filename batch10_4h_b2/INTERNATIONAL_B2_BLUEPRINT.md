# INTERNATIONAL B2 — FINAL NARROWED BLUEPRINT

## Title

**Evidence boundaries and transferability in selected operational-readiness domains: an evidence-informed integrative synthesis**

## Central question and objective

What can result-located evidence in selected operational-readiness domains support, and where must claims stop because of transferability and evidence-density limits? The objective is to synthesize bounded sleep/caffeine, cardiometabolic-transferability and task-specific performance evidence without presenting a global readiness model.

## Article type

Evidence-informed integrative synthesis; not a systematic review. The `PROPOSED SYNTHESIS FRAMEWORK — NOT YET VALIDATED` remains a conceptual organizer only.

## Claims and structure

- Supporting: task-dependent sleep/caffeine evidence; cardiometabolic transferability context; shooting null-result boundaries.
- Sections: evidence-boundary framing; selected domain evidence and transferability; limitations and future validation.
- Removed from core: nutrition/supplementation, physical fitness/academy readiness, hydration/heat.

## Density and viability

Four result IDs across three independent source families support a **short form** (estimated 2,500–3,500 words), not a broad full manuscript. All citations remain working-only and human review is pending.

## Limitations

Unadjudicated contradictory evidence (EV-0052, EV-0140, EV-1066) is not support. No global readiness score, framework validation, causal inference or Brazil-generalization is permitted.
