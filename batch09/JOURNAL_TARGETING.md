# Batch 09 — Journal targeting

## International
PRIMARY_EDITORIAL_TARGET: Journal of Occupational Health — Review Article.

Why:
- occupational-health audience;
- Review Article explicitly accommodates review/evaluation/commentary of research reports;
- 6,000-word maximum;
- structured abstract up to 250 words;
- 3–6 keywords;
- Key Points required;
- up to 6 figures/tables;
- explicit AI disclosure policy.

SECONDARY_EDITORIAL_TARGET: Frontiers in Public Health — Occupational Health and Safety.

## Brazil
PRIMARY_EDITORIAL_TARGET: Revista Brasileira de Saúde Ocupacional — Ensaio.

Why:
- direct Saúde do Trabalhador / SST scope;
- Ensaio permits conceptual/new-approach scientific discussion;
- 4,500-word limit and 40 references;
- Vancouver citations;
- Portuguese and English abstracts;
- AI-use declaration required;
- no publication fees.

The RBSO Review category was intentionally not selected because it is restricted to systematic reviews, meta-analyses, scoping reviews and bibliometric reviews with prior protocol.

SECONDARY_EDITORIAL_TARGET: Revista Brasileira de Medicina do Trabalho.
