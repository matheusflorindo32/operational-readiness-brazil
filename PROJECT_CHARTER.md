# Project Charter — Conceptual Essay Protocol v1.0

## Title
Do Atleta Tático à Prontidão Operacional: um framework aplicado de desempenho humano para a segurança pública brasileira

## English title
From Tactical Athlete to Operational Readiness: An Applied Human Performance Framework for Brazilian Public Safety

## Problem
Evidence relevant to human performance and operational readiness in Brazilian public safety is fragmented across physical capability, occupational performance, health, fatigue, cognition, load, injury, medical readiness, monitoring, and institutional contexts. There is an emerging need for a paradigm shift in attention to Brazilian public-safety professionals, moving from fragmented, episodic, and predominantly physical approaches toward an integrated, longitudinal, multidimensional, and implementation-oriented model of operational readiness, sustainable health, and occupational longevity.

## Central proposition
The **tactical athlete** construct is a conceptual starting point, not an endpoint. Operational readiness is the broader target state.

## Primary question
Which evidence-supported components, indicators, and implementation strategies can inform a feasible framework for assessing, developing, and maintaining operational readiness among Brazilian public safety professionals?

## Primary objective
Develop an evidence-informed applied framework for assessment, development, and maintenance of operational readiness in Brazilian public safety by critically integrating tactical-athlete concepts, human-performance evidence, Brazilian public-safety evidence, transferability considerations, and implementation-science principles.

## Design
**Conceptual and applied scientific essay informed by structured and reproducible evidence identification, critical synthesis, Brazilian contextual analysis, transferability assessment, and implementation-science concepts.**

This is not a systematic review, scoping review, meta-analysis, or experimental study.

## Evidence-identification architecture
Three complementary families are retained:
1. Tactical human performance and operational readiness.
2. APHT / medical readiness.
3. Implementation science.

The architecture is intended to support a rigorous conceptual essay and does not claim exhaustive systematic-review completeness.

## Implementation science
- Primary analytical framework for determinants/context: **CFIR**.
- Implementation outcomes: **Proctor Implementation Outcomes**.
- Subsidiary strategy taxonomy: **ERIC**.

## Transferability
Military and other tactical evidence is assessed explicitly for contextual transferability before it can inform Brazilian public-safety recommendations.

## Expected product
A **proposed evidence-informed Brazilian Operational Readiness Framework**, not a validated framework unless subsequent empirical validation is completed.

## Target journal
Strength & Conditioning Journal remains the primary journal positioning pending final fit reassessment for the essay format.
