# Project Charter — Protocol v1.0

## Title
Do Atleta Tático à Prontidão Operacional: um framework aplicado de desempenho humano para a segurança pública brasileira

## English title
From Tactical Athlete to Operational Readiness: An Applied Human Performance Framework for Brazilian Public Safety

## Problem
Evidence relevant to human performance and readiness in Brazilian public safety is fragmented across physical fitness, occupational performance, health, load, fatigue, cognition, injury, medical readiness and institutional sources. The project aims to synthesize and translate this evidence into a feasible, context-sensitive framework rather than merely describe the “tactical athlete” concept.

## Primary question
Which evidence-supported components, indicators, and implementation strategies can inform a feasible framework for assessing, developing, and maintaining operational readiness among Brazilian public safety professionals?

## Primary objective
Develop an evidence-informed applied framework for assessment, development and maintenance of operational readiness in Brazilian public safety by mapping national and international components, indicators and implementation strategies and evaluating their applicability to Brazil.

## PCC
- **Population:** operational public safety professionals; international law-enforcement/fire/first-response populations; military studies only as transferable evidence.
- **Concept:** operational readiness and human performance, including components, indicators, assessment methods, interventions and implementation conditions.
- **Context:** Brazilian public safety informed by comparable international tactical populations.

## Design
JBI scoping review + PRISMA-ScR + PRISMA-S + Brazilian contextual analysis + implementation mapping.

## Implementation science

- Outcomes: Proctor implementation outcomes.
- Subsidiary strategies: ERIC.
